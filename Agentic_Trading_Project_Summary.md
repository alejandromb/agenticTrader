# Agentic Trading -- Project Summary

## Vision

Agentic Trading is an engineering project to build an AI-assisted
investment research and portfolio management platform.

The objective is **not** to create an autonomous trader that blindly
buys and sells stocks. Instead, the goal is to build an evidence-driven
investment operating system that helps make higher-quality decisions
over time.

The philosophy is that improving the investment process should naturally
improve long-term results, while recognizing that no system can
eliminate uncertainty or guarantee market-beating returns.

## Guiding Principles

-   Treat investing as an engineering discipline.
-   Every investment must begin with a written thesis.
-   Separate evidence from opinion.
-   Optimize for decision quality, not trade frequency.
-   Focus on probabilities instead of predictions.
-   Respect risk above all else.
-   Continuously improve the system through measurement and post-mortem
    analysis.

Inspired by concepts from Nassim Taleb's *Fooled by Randomness*, the
project emphasizes avoiding hindsight bias, recognizing randomness, and
distinguishing skill from luck.

## Long-Term Goal

Build an AI Chief Investment Officer (AI CIO) capable of: - Researching
companies - Summarizing earnings reports - Reading financial
statements - Tracking macroeconomic events - Assessing portfolio risk -
Challenging investment ideas - Assisting with portfolio construction -
Integrating with Robinhood MCP for brokerage operations

The AI should function as an investment committee member rather than an
autonomous trader.

## Core Philosophy

The project is not about finding shortcuts to wealth.

It is about building a disciplined, transparent, continuously improving
investment decision system that combines human judgment with AI-assisted
research and analysis.

The long-term objective is to create an investment operating system that
becomes more capable with every decision, every review, and every
iteration.
